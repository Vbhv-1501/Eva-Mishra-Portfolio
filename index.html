01 — About
♪...where notes become prayers

A life woven with
ragas & rhythm

Hello, I'm Eva Mishra, a passionate music enthusiast and aspiring academician from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh. Music has always been more than just an art form for me — it is a way of expressing emotions, preserving culture, and connecting with people on a deeper level.

My journey in Hindustani Classical Music has shaped both my personality and my career aspirations. I pursued my higher studies in music from Bhatkhande Sanskriti Vishwavidyalay, Lucknow, and qualified UGC NET in Music, which further strengthened my goal of building a career in teaching and research.

Apart from academics, I actively share my passion for soulful and classical music through digital platforms and social media.

Currently, I am looking for new opportunities in the teaching field where I can contribute my knowledge, dedication, and passion for music while inspiring students through learning and performance.

"Music is the silence between the notes that speaks the loudest"
